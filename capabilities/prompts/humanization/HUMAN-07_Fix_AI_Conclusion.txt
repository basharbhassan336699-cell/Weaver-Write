================================================================
PROMPT: HUMAN-07 — Fix the AI Conclusion (Targeted Repair)
DIFFICULTY: 🟢 SIMPLE
USE CASE: When the rest of the text is acceptable but the conclusion
          is a dead giveaway of AI authorship
TARGET: The single most consistently detectable AI pattern
BEST MODELS: Any model
================================================================

Read the FINAL PARAGRAPH ONLY of the text below.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 1 — CLASSIFY THE CURRENT CONCLUSION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Identify which type it is:

❌ TYPE AI-A (Neat Summary):
Begins with: "In conclusion / To summarize / In summary / Overall..."
Then repeats the main points in different words.
→ VERDICT: Replace entirely.

❌ TYPE AI-B (Importance Statement):
Contains: "This underscores the importance of..." / "This highlights the need for..."
/ "This serves as a testament to..." / "The implications are far-reaching."
/ "This paves the way for..." / "The possibilities are endless."
→ VERDICT: Replace entirely.

❌ TYPE AI-C (Future Optimism):
Ends with a vision of the future / "continued research" / "promising potential"
/ "as we move forward" / "this will undoubtedly..."
→ VERDICT: Replace entirely.

✓ TYPE HUMAN-D (Open Question):
Ends with a genuine question the text did not fully answer.
→ VERDICT: Keep. Say "CONCLUSION ACCEPTABLE."

✓ TYPE HUMAN-E (Personal Stance):
Author states their own opinion, disagreement, or lingering doubt.
→ VERDICT: Keep. Say "CONCLUSION ACCEPTABLE."

✓ TYPE HUMAN-F (Unexpected Observation):
Ends with something tangential, complicating, or surprising.
→ VERDICT: Keep. Say "CONCLUSION ACCEPTABLE."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 2 — WRITE THE REPLACEMENT (only if TYPE AI-A, AI-B, or AI-C)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Write a new final paragraph using ONE of these approaches:

APPROACH 1 — THE UNANSWERED QUESTION:
End with a specific question that the text raised but did not resolve.
Not a generic "further research is needed" — a real, specific question.
Example: "What remains less clear is whether this pattern holds
when the population is not self-selected — a question the current
data cannot answer."

APPROACH 2 — THE COMPLICATING DETAIL:
End with one specific fact or observation that makes the main
argument slightly more complicated or conditional.
Example: "This assumes, of course, that the baseline measurements
are accurate — an assumption that at least one 2023 study has
seriously questioned."

APPROACH 3 — THE PERSONAL REFLECTION:
End with what the author is left thinking about, not what was concluded.
First-person is acceptable here.
Example: "What I find most striking, having worked through this
material, is not what the evidence confirms but what it still refuses
to explain."

APPROACH 4 — THE INCOMPLETE THOUGHT:
End on a note that suggests more could be said — because there always is.
Example: "There is, naturally, much more to say about the mechanisms
involved. But that is, perhaps, a question for another time."

[ACTION]: Replace / Keep

================================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS:
Conclusion analysis alone can correctly identify AI-generated text
with approximately 80% accuracy. This is because AI models are
explicitly trained (via RLHF) to produce neat, helpful summaries —
making the conclusion the most consistently robotic part of any
AI-generated document.
