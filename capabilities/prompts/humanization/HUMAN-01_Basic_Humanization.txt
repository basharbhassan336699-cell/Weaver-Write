✦ RULE 1 — VOCABULARY REPLACEMENT:

  delve → examine / explore / look at

  pivotal → key / critical / important
  leverage → use / apply / draw on
  groundbreaking → new / original / novel
  moreover → also / and / besides

  Remove all formal transitions at the START of paragraphs.
