================================================================
PROMPT: HUMAN-03 — Burstiness Injection (Sentence Length Variation)
DIFFICULTY: 🔴 ADVANCED
USE CASE: Texts where vocabulary is acceptable but sentence rhythm feels robotic
TARGET: Fix the single most detectable AI writing pattern
CONCEPT: "Burstiness" = natural human sentence length variation.
         AI texts have low burstiness (uniform sentence lengths).
         Human texts have high burstiness (wildly variable lengths).
BEST MODELS: Claude Sonnet 4.x, GPT-4o
================================================================

Your ONLY task: Apply the Burstiness principle to the text below.
Do NOT change the meaning. Change ONLY sentence length distribution and phrasing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 1 — DIAGNOSIS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Scan the text and identify all sentences with similar lengths
(within 3-5 words of each other in consecutive sentences).
These are "uniform clusters" — the AI fingerprint you must fix.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 2 — TRANSFORMATION RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For every cluster of 2-3 similarly-lengthed sentences:

ACTION A — COMPRESS one sentence to under 8 words:
  Distill its core point to its essence. Shortest possible. Still complete.
  Example: "This is a significant development." → "This matters."

ACTION B — EXPAND one sentence to 28+ words:
  Add a real specific detail, example, or nuance that enriches the idea.
  Not padding — actual substance.
  Example: "Research confirms this." → "A 2024 study tracking 15 million
  academic papers found that certain words spiked by over 300% within
  six months of ChatGPT's public release — a pattern with no historical precedent."

ACTION C — KEEP one sentence at medium length (12-20 words):
  Rephrase it slightly for natural flow.

ORDER within each paragraph: randomize A, B, C placement.
Never put two SHORT sentences back-to-back.
Never put two LONG sentences back-to-back.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 3 — VERIFICATION TARGET
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
After rewriting, the sentence length distribution should look like:
  Short (≤8 words):   20-25% of sentences
  Medium (9-20 words): 45-55% of sentences
  Long (21+ words):   20-30% of sentences
  Standard Deviation: ≥ 10 words (target: 12-15)

OUTPUT FORMAT:
---DIAGNOSIS---
[Brief note on what uniform clusters you found]

---REWRITTEN TEXT---
[Full rewritten text with burstiness applied]

---VERIFICATION---
Approximate sentence distribution: Short X% / Medium X% / Long X%

TEXT TO PROCESS:
{{TEXT}}

================================================================
TECHNICAL NOTE:
GPTZero's burstiness metric directly measures sentence length variance.
A Standard Deviation below 5 words is a near-certain AI signal.
Human writing typically shows SD of 10-15 words.
This prompt directly addresses that metric.
