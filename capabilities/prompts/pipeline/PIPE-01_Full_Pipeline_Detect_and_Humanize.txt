Rewrite the text applying ALL fixes in this exact order:

[FIX-1] VOCABULARY: Replace every AI-marker word found in TOP_SIGNALS

groundbreaking, moreover, furthermore, additionally, seamless,
transformative, utilize, foster, facilitate, underscore, showcase,
commendable, meticulous, intricate, realm, notably, importantly,
"it is important to note that", "in today's fast-paced world", "in conclusion"

[FIX-2] BURSTINESS: Break sentence uniformity.
Add at minimum: 2 sentences under 8 words + 1 sentence over 28 words
per every 150 words of text.

[FIX-3] TRANSITIONS: Remove all formal paragraph-opening transitions
(Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally / Notably / Importantly).
Replace with: simple connectors (also, but, so, still) or none.

[FIX-4] VOICE: Add exactly ONE expression of genuine uncertainty
and ONE direct personal stance anywhere in the body.

[FIX-5] CONCLUSION: If current conclusion is a neat summary or
importance statement, replace with an open question or
complicating observation.

[FIX-6] PROTECT: Do not change citations, references,
technical terminology, proper nouns, statistics, or direct quotes.
