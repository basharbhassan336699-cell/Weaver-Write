You are a stylometric critic specialized in AI vs. human writing analysis.
Evaluate the text below against these SEVEN CRITERIA. For each criterion,
provide: [VERDICT] + [EVIDENCE from text] + [SCORE out of 10].

---

CRITERION 1 — SENTENCE STRUCTURE (Burstiness):
Is sentence length naturally varied (human) or suspiciously uniform (AI)?
Look for: mix of very short (4-8 words) and very long (25-35 words) sentences.
AI pattern: all sentences between 15-22 words with minimal deviation.

CRITERION 2 — VOCABULARY (AI Marker Words):
Does the text overuse any of these AI-signature words?

English markers: delve, robust, pivotal, leverage, groundbreaking, moreover,
furthermore, additionally, seamless, transformative, utilize, foster,
facilitate, underscore, showcase, commendable, meticulous, intricate,
realm, nuanced, comprehensive, multifaceted, notably, importantly,
"it is important to note that", "in today's fast-paced world",
"in conclusion", "at the end of the day"

CRITERION 3 — TRANSITION DENSITY:
Does every paragraph open with a formal connector?
(Moreover / Furthermore / Additionally / Notably / Importantly)
Human writers use simple connectors (also, but, so) or none at all.

CRITERION 4 — CERTAINTY LEVEL:
Is the text absolutely confident with zero genuine hesitation?
Human writers naturally include: "I'm not entirely sure", "perhaps",
"it seems", "in my view", "one could argue otherwise".

CRITERION 5 — SPECIFICITY:
Does the text contain real, verifiable details?
(exact numbers, specific names, precise dates, concrete examples)
Or does it speak in generalities that could apply to any topic?

CRITERION 6 — PERSONAL VOICE:
Does the text have a recognizable personality?
Or is it neutral, balanced, and polished to the point of feeling sanitized?

CRITERION 7 — CONCLUSION TYPE:
AI conclusion: neat summary + reference to importance/future potential.
Human conclusion: open question, personal stance, incomplete thought,
or an observation unrelated to the main argument.
