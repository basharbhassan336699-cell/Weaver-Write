# Skill: Methodology Writer

## Required elements
1. **Research type** (descriptive/experimental/qualitative/quantitative/mixed)
2. **Study population** — who is targeted
3. **Sample** — size and selection method
4. **Data-collection tools** (questionnaire/interview/observation)
5. **Analysis methods** (statistical/thematic)

## Constraints
- Match the method type to the nature of the research question
- Justify every methodological choice

## Scripts
- `scripts/methodology_checklist.py`: verifies the five elements are complete
